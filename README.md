# 🏥 Smart Healthcare & Risk Factor Analysis Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Smart Healthcare & Risk Factor Analysis Dashboard** is a data visualization project developed using Tableau Public. The project focuses on exploring healthcare data to understand patient demographics, health conditions, lifestyle habits, and potential risk factors associated with different health outcomes.

The dashboard transforms healthcare data into interactive visualizations that make it easier to identify patterns, compare patient groups, and communicate meaningful insights.

This project demonstrates the use of data visualization and exploratory data analysis to present healthcare information in a clear and understandable format.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

The main objectives of this project are:

- Analyze the distribution of patients across different age groups.
- Understand the distribution of patients by gender.
- Explore patterns related to diabetes and heart disease.
- Examine blood pressure measurements across patient groups.
- Analyze Body Mass Index (BMI) and BMI categories.
- Explore relationships between lifestyle factors and health conditions.
- Study exercise levels and their distribution among patients.
- Examine smoking habits and alcohol consumption.
- Present healthcare information through interactive charts and a consolidated dashboard.

## 🧮 Understanding BMI and Blood Pressure

### Body Mass Index (BMI)

BMI is a measure calculated using a person's weight and height.

**Formula:**

BMI = Weight (kg) / [Height (m)]²

BMI can be used as a screening measure to categorize weight relative to height. However, it does not independently determine a person's overall health.

### Blood Pressure

Blood pressure measures the force of blood against artery walls. It is generally recorded using two values:

- **Systolic pressure:** Pressure when the heart contracts.
- **Diastolic pressure:** Pressure when the heart relaxes between beats.

The dashboard uses the blood pressure fields available in the dataset to explore recorded measurements and their distribution.
